- `rlm.set_cost_ceiling(cost_usd=None, *, spawn_limit=None)` — an opt-in cap on what one session's spawn tree may spend (the parent's own usage and every attributed child's usage included). While a USD ceiling is set, `rlm.spawn` refuses models the catalog has no price for and any refused spawn raises `RuntimeError` before the child starts; a `spawn_limit` caps admissions as the hard backstop. Returns the ledger snapshot with numeric fields.
- The ceilings and the admission count persist in the session log, so a worker restart or reattach restores them instead of silently uncapping the tree; the spend rebuilds from every recorded branch.
- A live model switch re-evaluates whether inherited-model spawns run on a priced model.
- The cap covers the whole spawn tree: each child inherits what is left of its parent's budget at spawn time (and hands what is left of that to its own children); a child can tighten its inherited budget with `rlm.set_cost_ceiling` but never loosen it.
