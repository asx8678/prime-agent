- Experimental context folding, off by default. With `contextFolding.enabled: true` in settings, the model can wrap a sub-task in `await fold.begin(description, goal)` and `await fold.end(result)` (the bundled `fold` skill); later requests leave out the work between them and keep the result, while the transcript and session log keep everything. `contextFolding.maxFoldTokens` (default 32768) sets when an open fold is asked to close. Session telemetry adds fold counts and the estimated tokens folded, never content.
